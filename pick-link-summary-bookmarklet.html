Pick a link, get a summary

Summarize where a link leads without opening it, or the YouTube video you’re watching.

Summarize

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

On a web page, click the bookmarklet, then click the link you want summarized. You can also choose “This page” in the small banner. Press Escape to cancel.

On YouTube, choose “This video” to summarize the video you’re watching from its transcript, or click any video link to summarize that video instead. The video needs captions, either added by its creator or generated automatically by YouTube.

Links on the current site are fetched by your browser and converted to a compact Markdown-style document. Links on other sites go through Jina Reader for extraction and Markdown conversion. The extracted text is sent to ch.at. Do not use it for private pages. YouTube transcripts are read from YouTube by your browser and also sent to ch.at. This only works on youtube.com itself: other sites aren’t allowed to read YouTube pages, so for a YouTube link elsewhere the bookmarklet offers to open the video instead. Sites can block bookmarklets or the requests they make; on those sites, open the link and try the “This page” option.
