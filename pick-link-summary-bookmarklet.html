Summarize

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

On a web page, click the bookmarklet, then click the link you want summarized. You can also choose “This page” in the small banner. Press Escape to cancel.

Links on the current site are fetched by your browser and converted to a compact Markdown-style document. Links on other sites go through Jina Reader for extraction and Markdown conversion. The extracted text is sent to ch.at. Do not use it for private pages. Sites can block bookmarklets or the requests they make; on those sites, open the link and try the “This page” option.
