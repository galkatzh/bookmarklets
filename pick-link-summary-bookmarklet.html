Summarize links and videos

Summarize where a link leads without opening it, or the YouTube video you’re watching, then ask follow-up questions.

Summarize

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

  1. On a web page, click the bookmark, then click the link you want summarized. Choose “This page” in the banner to summarize the page you’re on instead. Press Escape to cancel.
  2. On a YouTube video, clicking the bookmark summarizes the video from its transcript right away. Clicking a YouTube link on any other page summarizes that video.
  3. Under the summary, ask a question about the full text. Replace the question to ask another; earlier answers aren’t kept.

Long transcripts are split into parts of up to 128,000 characters, summarized one by one, and combined. The question box appears when the source is short enough to send in full.

Links on the current site are converted to Markdown in your browser. Links to other sites go through Jina Reader. For YouTube, the bookmarklet uses the captions on the video’s page when it can, and otherwise the YouTube Transcript API, so videos need captions. The extracted text is sent to ch.at. Don’t use it on private pages; some sites’ security settings block bookmarklets or the requests they make.
